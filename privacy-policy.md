# Nocturn Privacy Policy

Nocturn is a monitoring client for your own self-hosted Hermes agent.

- Nocturn collects no data. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
- All network traffic goes exclusively to the Hermes dashboard address you
  configure in Settings — typically a machine on your own network.
- Connection settings are stored only on your device.
